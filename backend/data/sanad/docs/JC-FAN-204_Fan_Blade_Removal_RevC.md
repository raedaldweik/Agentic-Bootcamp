# JC-FAN-204 Engine fan blade removal

## 1. Applicability and authorisation

This job card covers removal of the stage 1 fan blades from a TF-35 engine at the Fan Module Bay. Revision C is the latest approved revision and supersedes revision B. Work to any earlier revision is not permitted.

The task needs a crew of two technicians qualified for TF-35 fan blade removal and inspection, and about 14 working hours. It may start only when all six readiness checks are complete and the supervisor has released the work: work authorisation, job card at the latest approved revision, technician qualification, tool T-117 at the gate, work area confirmed, and safety precautions confirmed.

## Key care point

Avoid damaging the O-ring and adjacent parts. The spinner O-ring seat and the neighbouring blades are easily marked during spinner removal and blade extraction. Fit the protective cover kit before any tool goes near the fan.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 2. Tools

Six tools are required. All six must be on the Gate 1 tool board or in the Gate 1 bay before the task starts. No substitution is permitted for any of them.

| Tool | Name | Used for | Calibration |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-117 | Fan blade extractor | Pulling each blade out of its disk slot | Required |
| T-208 | Lock plate removal tool | Releasing the blade retaining lock plates | Not required |
| T-063 | Torque wrench, 50-250 lbf-in | Removing and refitting the spinner fasteners | Required |
| T-311 | Blade support fixture | Supporting the blade so the root is not damaged | Not required |
| T-529 | Padded blade storage rack | Keeping the removed blades in slot order | Not required |
| K-014 | Protective cover kit | Covering the fan case liner, spinner and adjacent blades | Not required |

Check the calibration label on T-117 and T-063 before use. A tool whose calibration has lapsed must not be used and must be returned to tool control.

## 3. Safety precautions

Confirm the engine is secured in the stand and the stand brakes are applied. Wear cut-resistant gloves when handling blades: leading and trailing edges are sharp. Keep the work area clear of loose items; anything dropped into the fan case must be reported and recovered before work continues. Never lever against the fan disk.

<!-- page 3 -->
## 4. Procedure

Follow the steps in order and confirm each one on the job card before moving on.

Step 1. Verify before starting. Confirm the work is released, revision C is at the work stand, and all six tools are on the Gate 1 tool board, including T-117.

Step 2. Protect the work area. Fit the protective cover kit (K-014) over the fan case liner, spinner and adjacent blades. Position the padded blade storage rack (T-529) beside the stand.

Step 3. Remove the spinner. Remove the spinner fasteners with the torque wrench (T-063) in the sequence shown. Lift the spinner clear without touching the O-ring seat. Bag and tag the fasteners and discard the O-ring.

Step 4. Release the lock plates. Release the blade retaining lock plates with the lock plate removal tool (T-208), one blade position at a time. Do not lever against the disk.

Step 5. Extract the blades. Support each blade with the blade support fixture (T-311) and pull it from its disk slot with the fan blade extractor (T-117). Place each blade on the rack in slot order.

Step 6. Inspect and record. Inspect each blade against Inspection Standards Manual Chapter 72, Revision F, Table 72-21-3. Record any finding with depth, length and position. Do not disposition a finding.

<!-- page 4 -->
## 5. Findings and sign-off

A finding is recorded by the technician who made it, with the blade serial number, the zone, and the measured depth, length and position. The technician does not accept, reject or disposition a finding. Disposition belongs to an authorised Engineering or Quality role under the Approval Authority Matrix (QP-014).

Where a measurement is above the limit for its zone, work on that blade stops and the blade is tagged and held. Removal of the remaining blades may continue.

The task is complete when all blades are on the rack in slot order, every blade has been inspected and the inspection is recorded. The technician confirms completion on the job card. Certification of the work is by certifying staff.
