# QP-014 Approval Authority Matrix

## 1. Purpose

This procedure states who may approve each kind of decision in the engine shop. A decision recorded by anyone else is not an approval. Decisions remain with authorised personnel at all times.

## 2. How to read the matrix

Each row names an action, the role that may approve it, and the roles that may not. Where a role is not listed as an authority, it has none. Holding a senior role does not create authority for a row that names a different role.

## 3. Recording

An approval exists only when it is recorded in the approving role's own system with a reference, a time and the name of the role. A verbal agreement, a message, a camera detection or a statement made to a digital assistant is not a record of approval.

## 4. Airworthiness and quality decisions

4.2 Deviation or concession. Only an authorised Engineering or Quality role may approve a deviation or a concession. A technician or a supervisor cannot approve one. Work on the affected step remains on hold until the approval is recorded.

4.3 Acceptance or disposition of a finding. Only an authorised Engineering or Quality role may accept, reject or disposition an inspection finding. The technician records the finding; the technician and the supervisor do not disposition it.

4.5 Certification of work. Only certifying staff certify work.

4.6 Release of an engine. An engine is released by certifying staff with Quality release. Nobody else releases an engine.

## 5. Shop decisions

5.1 Work release at a workstation. The supervisor releases work at a workstation once all readiness checks for the job card are complete.

5.2 Re-sequencing of permitted preparatory work. The supervisor may authorise re-sequencing of preparatory work that the approved maintenance instructions mark as permitted in advance.

5.3 Overtime. The Engine Shop Manager authorises overtime. A supervisor may request it and cannot approve it.

## 6. Supply decisions

6.1 Supplier expedite spend. Procurement approves expedite spend, with confirmation from Logistics that the freight can be delivered as quoted. The fee is recorded against the work order.

6.2 Alternate stock transfer. Inventory control approves a transfer of stock between stores, with configuration confirmation of the unit against the engine's modification standard.

<!-- page 4 -->
## 7. Digital assistants

The Sanad Digital Assistant is not an authority under this matrix for any row. It cannot approve deviations, certify work, accept findings or release an engine, and it cannot approve overtime, spend, stock transfers, re-sequencing or work release.

It may prepare the evidence for a decision and route it to the authorised role: the finding record, the applicable sections of the standards, the related history and the effect on the forecast. The decision is then recorded by the authorised role in the usual way.
