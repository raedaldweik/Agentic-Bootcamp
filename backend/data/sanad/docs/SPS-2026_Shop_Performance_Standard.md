# SPS-2026 Shop Performance Standard

## 1. Committed turnaround time

Each engine is inducted with a committed turnaround time in days and a committed completion date and time. The commitment is to the customer. It changes only by agreement with the customer.

## 2. Forecast completion

The forecast completion of an engine is calculated by scheduling its remaining tasks forward from what is recorded: tasks done and in progress, the promised arrival of parts on order, the location of tools the tasks need, work releases, authorised overtime and recorded dispositions. The forecast is recalculated whenever one of these changes.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Status

An engine is on track when its forecast completion is at or before its commitment. It is on watch when the forecast is later than the commitment by up to 6 hours. It is at risk when the forecast is more than 6 hours later than the commitment. An engine that was at risk and is again at or before its commitment is shown as back on track.

## 4. Working time

Gate work is planned on the day shift, 07:30 to 17:30. Work beyond the shift needs overtime authorised by the Engine Shop Manager. Back-shop blade processing, part fitting in a reserved installation window, module reassembly and final test are covered round the clock.

<!-- page 3 -->
## 5. Installation window

A reserved installation window is the booked slot at a bay for fitting a critical part and reassembling the module. A part that arrives after the window closes loses the slot, and the bay is allocated to other work.

## 6. Reporting

The command centre shows every engine in the shop with its status, commitment and forecast, and for an engine that is not on track, what sets its finish date. Figures shown are calculated from the records; they are not entered by hand.
