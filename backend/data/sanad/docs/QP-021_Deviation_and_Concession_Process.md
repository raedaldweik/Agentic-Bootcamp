# QP-021 Deviation and Concession Process

## 1. When this applies

A deviation is a request to depart from an approved instruction before the work is done. A concession is a request to accept a condition outside limits after it is found. Both need approval by an authorised Engineering or Quality role under QP-014 section 4.2. Neither can be approved by a technician, a supervisor or a digital assistant.

## 2. Hold

From the moment a condition outside limits is recorded, work on the affected step is on hold. Work that does not depend on the affected item may continue. The hold is lifted only by a recorded approval or disposition.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Evidence package

The request is made with an evidence package that contains: the finding record with the measurements as taken; the applicable sections of the Inspection Standards Manual and the job card; the related history of the component; and the current effect on the engine's forecast completion.

The evidence package may be prepared by the person raising the request or by the Sanad Digital Assistant on their behalf. Preparing and routing the package is not an approval and changes nothing in the plan.

## 4. Review and decision

The authorised Engineering or Quality role reviews the package and records one of: accept as is, repair to an approved scheme, or reject. The decision is recorded with a reference. Until it is recorded the request shows as pending.

## 5. After the decision

A repair disposition can add work to the plan: the repair itself, a re-inspection slot and any consumable material. The forecast is recalculated when the disposition is recorded, and the recovery plan is reassessed against it.
