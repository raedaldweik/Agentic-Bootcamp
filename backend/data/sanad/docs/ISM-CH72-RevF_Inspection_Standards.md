# Inspection Standards Manual, Chapter 72: Fan section

## 72-21 Fan 1 blades: general

Every fan 1 blade removed from an engine is inspected visually over its whole surface and dimensionally where damage is seen. The blade is identified by its serial number before any measurement is taken, so that a result is never recorded against the wrong blade.

The leading edge is divided into three zones for assessment. Zone A is the tip: the outer span of the leading edge. Zone B is mid-span. Zone C is the root: the inner span of the leading edge, down to the blade base.

<!-- page 2 -->
## Table 72-21-3 Leading-edge scratch limits

| Zone | Location | Serviceable limit (depth) | If the limit is exceeded |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, tip | Leading edge, outer span | 0.25 mm or less | Measure depth, length and position; raise a finding |
| B, mid-span | Leading edge, mid span | 0.25 mm or less | Measure depth, length and position; raise a finding |
| C, root | Leading edge, inner span | 0.25 mm or less | Measure depth, length and position; raise a finding |

A scratch at or below 0.25 mm depth in any zone is serviceable without repair and is recorded as within limits.

A scratch deeper than 0.25 mm is outside the serviceable limit. Measure its depth, length and position, record a finding, tag and hold the blade. Disposition is by an authorised Engineering or Quality role. The technician does not disposition.

## Root zone caution

Check the blade base whenever a scratch is found in zone C. A crack at the blade base requires rejection of the blade regardless of scratch size.

<!-- page 3 -->
## 72-21-08 Approved repair: leading-edge blend

A leading-edge scratch above the serviceable limit may be blended to repair scheme RS-72-21-08 when an authorised Engineering or Quality role issues that disposition. The repair uses the leading-edge blend kit BK-72-08 and is followed by fluorescent penetrant re-inspection. A blended blade is moment weighed again and the fan balance is verified at final test.

A blend repair is never started on the strength of a recorded finding alone. It needs the recorded disposition.

## Recording

Each finding records the engine, the job card, the blade serial number, the damage type, and the measured result against the limit for each zone inspected. Measurements are recorded as taken; they are not rounded to the limit.
