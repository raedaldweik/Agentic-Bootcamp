# Approved Maintenance Instructions: TF-35 Fan Module

## 1. Scope

These instructions govern disassembly, inspection, repair and reassembly of the TF-35 fan module. They are read together with the job card for each task. Where a job card and these instructions differ, stop and raise it with the supervisor; do not choose between them.

## 2. Sequence of work

The fan module is worked in this order: fan blade removal and inspection (JC-FAN-204); blade processing in the back shop, which is cleaning, non-destructive testing, any approved repair, moment weighing and blade set mapping; fitting of the front fan retaining ring P-4582 (JC-FAN-231); fan module reassembly (JC-FAN-232); and final test and release.

Blade processing normally takes about 110 hours of continuous back-shop time for a full blade set. An approved blend repair with re-inspection adds about 8 hours.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Fan retaining ring P-4582

The front fan retaining ring P-4582 must be fitted before the fan module is reassembled. There is no approved substitute part. A ring taken from stock must have its configuration confirmed against the engine's modification standard before it is issued; a serviceable tag alone is not enough.

Fitting the ring takes about 2 hours. It is done in the reserved installation window at the Fan Module Bay.

## 4. Fan module reassembly

Blades are refitted in the mapped slot order from the moment weighing record. Reassembly takes about 4 hours. About 2 of those hours are preparatory work that does not need the ring to be fitted: kitting, torque tool set-up and laying out the mapped blade set. With the supervisor's authorisation this preparatory work may be done in advance while the ring is in transit.

<!-- page 3 -->
## 5. Final test and release

Final test and release takes about 4 hours. About 2 of those hours are preparation that may be done in advance with the supervisor's authorisation: fitting the test adaptor and assembling the documentation pack. Where a blade has had a blend repair, a fan balance verification of about half an hour is added.

Release of the engine is by certifying staff with Quality release. Nobody else, and no system, releases an engine.

## 6. Care points

Avoid damaging the O-ring and adjacent parts during spinner removal and refit. Fit a new spinner O-ring set (P-2210) at every refit. Do not reuse a lock plate (P-3391) that is outside its inspection limits.
